FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Common questions
Quick answers to help you work faster.
How do I add a new job? ＋

Open Add Job from the sidebar, enter the opportunity details and save it. The job will then appear in your applications and pipeline.

How does the pipeline work? ＋

Your pipeline represents the journey of each opportunity. Jobs can move from Saved to Applied, Screening, Interview and Offer, while unsuccessful opportunities can be marked Rejected.

What is the difference between Applications and Pipeline? ＋

Applications gives you a detailed list of your opportunities, while Pipeline provides a visual board for understanding where each opportunity currently stands.

How are my analytics calculated? ＋

Analytics are calculated from the jobs you have recorded in JobFlow and their current pipeline statuses. As your data grows, your performance metrics become more meaningful.

Can I edit a job after saving it? ＋

Yes. Open the job details page and select Edit Job to update its information, notes or current status.
